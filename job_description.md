Job Description:

College/University Graduate hires (within 12 months of graduation per the company's definition) undergoing a specific training and development path within the EMEA Graduate Program.

What you’ll do:

As a Data Analytics Intern, you will work within the Channel Team to support the design, development, and maintenance of reports, dashboards, and forecasts that help drive business decisions. Your role will be pivotal in turning data into meaningful insights that support strategy and performance tracking.

Your responsibilities will include:

Assisting in the collection, cleaning, and analysis of business data.

Designing and maintaining Power BI dashboards to visualize KPIs, trends, and forecasts.

Supporting the creation of regular reporting packages for management and internal stakeholders.

Performing ad hoc analysis to answer specific business questions or validate hypotheses.

Collaborating with cross-functional teams to ensure accurate and timely data flows.

What you’ll need:

A background in Data Science, Statistics, Economics, Business Analytics or a related field.

Advanced proficiency in Power BI – ability to build interactive dashboards from scratch and manage data models.

Good command of Excel and data analysis tools.

Basic understanding of data visualization principles.

Analytical mindset and attention to detail.

What we’d prefer you bring:

Curiosity, a problem-solving attitude, and an eagerness to learn.

Ability to interpret and communicate data in a clear and compelling way.

Interest in business processes and channel performance.

What you can expect from us:

A practical learning experience working on real-world datasets

Access to mentorship from data analysts and business professionals.

Exposure to tools, techniques, and business intelligence best practices.

Opportunity to build a strong foundation in reporting, forecasting, and dashboard creation.

A supportive, collaborative environment that fosters innovation and growth.
