## Job Description

Software Engineering Intern - Fullstack (x/f/m) Set a new pulse for healthcare!We are looking for a Software Engineering Intern to join the Engineering team.Your mission will be to build innovative products and features that improve the daily lives of care teams and patients. You will work in a feature team developing solutions that directly impact healthcare professionals and patients across Europe, contributing to our Operating System powered by AI to manage all clinical and administrative activities.Working in the tech team at Doctolib means building innovative products and features to improve the daily lives of care teams and patients.What you'll doYour responsibilities include but are not limited to:
Build and own features alongside experienced engineers — from design and development to testing and shipping — with a direct impact on real users
Collaborate with engineers, product managers, and designers to solve complex technical challenges and build scalable, secure, and user-friendly healthcare tools
Write clean, efficient code following industry best practices in design, testing, and peer review
Solve real-world problems that directly enhance patient care and improve doctor-patient relationships
Explore new tools and technologies, including AI and distributed systems, as part of your continuous growth
Contribute to a culture of learning and collaboration by sharing knowledge and engaging actively with your team
